Euro-Office, a European alternative to Microsoft 365 built by Nextcloud, IONOS,
Open-Xchange and Office.eu, will soon ship native desktop apps for Windows, Linux
and macOS instead of running only in the browser.

**My comment.** The EU is trying to become independent from the United States, and
it starts in the right place: office software. That is the low-hanging fruit. Office
suites are mature, well understood and easy to replace, and every public institution
pays for them, so the savings are large and the risk is small.

This is only the first step. With AI, rewriting the rest of our software stack is no
longer a decade-long project. Europe can now afford to move much more of its digital
infrastructure onto its own, open tools — and it should do so quickly.
