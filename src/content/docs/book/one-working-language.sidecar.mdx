## Open questions

- **ASK-1** (`direct-democracy-dump.md:153`; chat 2026-09-22; R4 contradiction 3). The dump demands English as the Union's official language; the chat and `political-path.md:102-108` start with one state or Central Europe; `direct-democracy.md:193-194` says a state alone is a target. The chapter assumes both (Union-wide demand, regional start). If the ask is one of them, the section "Where it starts" changes.
- **ASK-2** (`book/language.md:10-12`). "Second official language in every EU member state" (2024) versus "official working language of business and administration" (2026). The chapter uses the narrower phrase.

## Research that did not make the page

- **Cost of EU multilingualism.** No verified figure was found on the European Parliament language-policy fact sheet or the EU languages page during this task; the objection is answered without a number. If a figure is wanted, it needs a primary source (DG Translation or DG Interpretation annual figures), not a press quote.
- **Malta, language of the courts.** Article 5 of the Maltese constitution makes Maltese the language of the courts unless Parliament provides for English; the chapter cites the administration clause only.
- **Ireland's experience.** Irish is the first official language by constitution and English the second; that the national language declined anyway is the honest counter-example to "enhancing, not replacing", and belongs beside the Irish/Maltese objection if the chapter ever argues that national languages are safe. Not used because no census figure was verified in this task.
- **NATO STANAG 6001** (the allied language-proficiency standard) would sharpen the offered military argument; not verified, not used.
- **Distributional effects** (`european-english.sidecar.mdx:46-47`): used in the box against the chapter; the rest of that sidecar is the standard's research and stays there.
- **Salvaged homepage cards** (`.plan/reports/2026-09-25-salvaged-from-homepage.md:102, 219, 293-311`): slogans superseded by the frame's mechanism; not restored.
