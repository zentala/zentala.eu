## Source checks, 2026-09-25

Fetched and confirmed for this draft:

- Swiss popular initiative: 100,000 signatures within 18 months; jus cogens and
  unity-of-subject validity conditions; counter-proposal — Wikipedia, *Popular
  initiative in Switzerland* (Fedlex itself needs JavaScript and could not be
  fetched; the article numbers are cited from the earlier draft's reading of
  Fedlex).
- Optional referendum: 50,000 signatures or eight cantons within 100 days —
  Wikipedia, *Optional referendum*.
- Swiss electorate 22 September 2024: 5,611,289 — Wikipedia, *2024 Swiss
  referendums* (Federal Chancellery data).
- Voting frequency and turnout: about four times a year, average fifteen
  questions, turnout a little over 40% — aboutswitzerland.eda.admin.ch.
- 192 federal initiatives voted 1893–2014, 22 adopted — Wikipedia, *Popular
  initiative in Switzerland*. The earlier draft's "over 200 voted, around 10 per
  cent adopted" (ch.ch) returned a 404 and was replaced.
- Venice Commission CDL-AD(2022)015, III.7: advises against turnout and approval
  quorums; approval quorum or special majority acceptable for matters of
  fundamental constitutional significance; multiple majorities acceptable in
  federal states — read from the PDF.
- Poland, Constitution Art. 125: binding if more than half of those entitled
  to vote take part — constituteproject.org (sejm.gov.pl is behind a CAPTCHA).
- Poland, registered voters at the 2024 European election: 29,098,155 —
  Wikipedia (PKW data). Eurostat's eligible-population estimate (demo_popep)
  is 32,148,844 — a different base.
- ECI: one million signatures, seven member states, twelve months, organiser
  group of seven, Commission replies within six months and is not obliged to
  legislate — citizens-initiative.europa.eu.
- Women's suffrage: 1959 rejection (33–66), February 1971 federal vote,
  31 October 1971 first federal election, Appenzell Innerrhoden compelled by
  the Federal Supreme Court on 27 November 1990 — Wikipedia.

Not re-verified (kept from the earlier draft with its source link):

- Act of 14 March 2003 on the national referendum, Art. 63 (500,000-citizen
  motion; Sejm not obliged) — ISAP is behind a CAPTCHA.
- The Union electorate. The European Parliament results site publishes turnout
  (50.74%) but not the electorate; Eurostat's demo_popep gives per-country
  eligible-population estimates and no EU total in the API response fetched.
  The chapter rounds to "≈ 360 million" and labels the Union row accurate to a
  few per cent.

  default (2026-09-25) is the narrow core, "less rather than more"; the chapter
  applies it as a working default and says so.
