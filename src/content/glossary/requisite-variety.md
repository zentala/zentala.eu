Ross Ashby's law; Stafford Beer applied it to organisations and states. The
definition itself is Ashby's — attribution to Paweł's voice is an open
question (IA §3). See [Steering](/book/steering#a-controller-must-correct-at-least-as-fast-as-the-thing-it-steers).
