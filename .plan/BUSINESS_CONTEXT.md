# Business context

## Project

EU Reform Vision is a public working project proposing practical European integration through shared language, reusable digital public software and industrial capabilities.

## Current strategic thesis

Europe loses scale when every country builds separate processes, software and industrial capacity. The project explores voluntary standards and shared capabilities that preserve national autonomy while making cross-border cooperation easier.

## Important distinctions

- Public proposals are not official EU policy.
- Sidecars preserve hypotheses and research material; they are not published reports.
- Digital integration is primarily an institutional and scale problem, not a specific technology choice.
- Robotisation discussions should distinguish agricultural automation, industrial components and defence-related dual-use research.
