# Goals

## North-star goal

Develop a credible, testable and contributor-friendly vision for European integration in which shared institutions, software and industrial capabilities create concrete benefits for people across member states.

## Strategic goals

- Make the site's purpose and hierarchy understandable within two clicks from the homepage.
- Turn each pillar into a repeatable sequence: public charter → research method → implementation proposal → follow-ups.
- Establish European software development as a strategic integration capability, with interoperable public solutions that countries can adopt voluntarily.
- Keep the benefits of digitalisation and robotisation distributed toward citizens and public capacity, not captured only by incumbent corporations.
- Explore Central European roles in software, advanced components and dual-use industrial systems without confusing hypotheses with established strategy.
- Preserve evidence, uncertainty and discarded alternatives so future contributors can continue the work.

## Success signals

- A new reader can find the main thesis, pillars and current work without getting lost in legacy URLs.
- Every active pillar has at least one public proposal and one internal implementation track.
- Claims are linked to evidence or explicitly marked as hypotheses.
- Contributors can pick a bounded task without reconstructing the project from conversation history.
