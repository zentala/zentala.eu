selection rather than by refusal; (2) a European education programme for the global south,
as taking responsibility instead of importing people; (3) shortening the working week as
the way to make lifelong learning real, funded by the work AI and robots take away.

## Co z tego wynika dla strony (agent's reading)

1. **Chapter: migration answered by education and selection.** Free online education in
   European languages and skills; clear published criteria for who may come and work;
   selection by demonstrated effort rather than by arrival; remote European degrees.
2. **Chapter: taking responsibility for the global south.** A translation-scale education
   programme — gardening, orchards, desert farming, first aid, basic medicine, basic
   construction — machine-translated into hundreds of languages; possibly tools for
   self-organisation and direct democracy; a deliberate return path, people trained here
   for a few years and going back with something.
3. **Chapter: the shorter week as lifelong learning.** Cut the week where AI and robots
   take the work; minimum wage redefined as four days; the fifth paid on top; weekend work
   priced higher; people free to choose three long days and four for their own projects.
4. **Terminology rule for the whole site**: use the established terms (semi-direct
   democracy, citizens' initiative, optional referendum, brake and accelerator) and teach
   them, because education is part of the goal. Keep "direct democracy" as the headline
   phrase because it names what is being added.
5. **Thresholds**: his instruction is 2% initiative / 1% veto. Confirm and apply.
6. **Voice**: he is an activist, not a politician, and says so. The site should ask for
   help rather than pretend to know: "I do not know how to do this; if you do, tell me."
