# Chapter ideas — the map of what is started, half-written or only said

The index Paweł asked for: every stub, every idea pulled out of a page, every subject
mentioned once and not yet written. Nothing lives only in a chat log. Linked from
`.plan/BACKLOG.md`.

**Three places material can sit, and the rule for each:**

| Place | What belongs there |
|---|---|
| `<chapter>.sidecar.mdx` next to a chapter | working notes for a chapter that EXISTS (`draft: true`, excluded from `/book`) — e.g. `src/content/docs/book/european-english.sidecar.mdx` |
| `.plan/vision/*.md` | Paweł's verbatim dumps, kept as source so writing never replaces his words |
| this file | subjects with no chapter yet, and passages cut from a page that have no home |

- A chapter written: move its row to the top table with its source dump.
