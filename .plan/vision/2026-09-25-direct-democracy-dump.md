# Brain dump, 2026-09-25 — direct democracy, the digital state, the playbook

Paweł dictated this. It is recorded verbatim (Polish, as spoken) because it is source
material, not instructions: the writing built from it must not quietly replace his
positions with smoother ones. Chapters drawing on it link back here.

Covers four things the site did not have: (1) the digitalisation argument together with
its danger — a digital state is a total state; (2) direct democracy as the balance of
power that makes digitalisation safe, demanded across the whole EU at every level;
(3) the playbook — what citizens should demand and in what order; (4) why he wrote any
of this: a positivist answer to complaint.
