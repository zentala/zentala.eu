## Co z tego wynika dla strony (agent's reading, not Paweł's words)

1. **Safeguards page.** Map the Swiss safeguards item by item and propose the EU/Polish
   equivalent beside each. Must be verified against sources, not written from memory.
2. **Binding referendums, Swiss thresholds scaled proportionally to population.**
3. **Paper first, digital in parallel as learning.** Paper as a permanent fallback for the
   basic functions of the state, not only for voting. Voting in schools and town halls.
4. **Technocrats propose, citizens intervene at any stage and approve or reject** — NOT a
   menu of priced options. Correct the earlier formulation.
5. **European Citizens' Initiative**: a practical how-to — registration, organiser
   committee, seven member states, signature collection, the online system, the deadline.
6. **Identity**: doing this together is itself a European identity-building act. "Europe of
   nations" that shows how much we share.
7. **Above parties, not a new party.** The real divide is whether one wants to give power
   to people. It tests who is actually a democrat.
8. **Union of Nations rebuilds the EU from within — it does not replace it.**
9. **AI section for the manifesto**: AI takes work; the freed time can go into building the
   state; print money to pay people to build the robotics and the digital state rather than
   sending them to war; politicians deliver what we expect, so raise expectations; business
   incentives favour slow modernisation.
10. **Why there is no European AI**: no European science working shoulder to shoulder, and
    no shared language. Language integration is upstream of European AI and robotics.
