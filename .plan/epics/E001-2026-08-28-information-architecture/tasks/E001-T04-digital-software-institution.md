# E001-T04: Reframe digital integration around European public software

## Objective

Describe a European capability for designing, funding, maintaining and sharing reusable public software, with voluntary adoption and room for competing implementations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Explain the institutional gap and the scale economics.
- [ ] Define reusable building blocks, governance and procurement principles.
- [ ] Show how different national laws can use similar processes and software.
- [ ] Identify benefits for citizens, public bodies, businesses and startups.
- [ ] Separate principles from technology choices.
