# E001-T02: Research — Liquid Democracy, Participatory Systems & Weighted Voting

## Goal

Research real implementations of liquid democracy, citizens' assemblies, sortition, and weighted voting systems. The author wants monthly digital votes on everything from EU directives to school principals — this research documents what models exist and what works.

## Context from Author

The vision: votes on multiple levels (EU, national, regional, municipal, institutional — hospital director, police chief, school principal). Citizens can propose referenda on-demand. Liquid democracy for ministers (citizens elect, can recall). Internal democracy within organizations (doctors vote on hospital matters). Weighted votes across stakeholders.

## Questions You MUST Answer

**Liquid democracy:**
- What is liquid democracy? (definition, how delegation works)
- Where has it been implemented? (LiquidFeedback in Germany Pirate Party; others?)
- What were outcomes? (participation, quality of decisions)
- Can liquid democracy scale to national level? (evidence, experiments)
- What are the failure modes? (cascade delegation, power concentration)

**Citizens' assemblies & sortition:**
- Ireland Citizens' Assembly: what topics, how selected, what outcomes? (abortion, climate, housing)
- France Convention Citoyenne pour le Climat: topic, process, recommendations, government adoption rate
- What other countries used sortition? (Iceland constitution, Belgium G1000)
- What is the evidence for quality of decisions vs. elected legislatures?

**On-demand referenda:**
- How do petition-to-referendum systems work? (thresholds, timeline)
- Which countries allow citizens to force a parliamentary debate? (UK e-petitions, etc.)
- What is the "right of initiative" and where does it exist?

**Weighted voting & institutional democracy:**
- Academic research on weighted voting in organizations
- Examples of workers having voting rights in company governance (Germany Mitbestimmung)
- Hospital/school governance with staff voting — any models?
- What is "demarchy" or multi-stakeholder governance?

**Digital platforms:**
- Pol.is (used in Taiwan vTaiwan): how it works, what topics, outcomes
- Decidim (Barcelona, others): participation rates, type of issues
- Consul (Madrid): results, adoption in other cities
