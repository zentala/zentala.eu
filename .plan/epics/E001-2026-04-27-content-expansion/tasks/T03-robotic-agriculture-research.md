# E001-T03: Research — Robotic Agriculture, Polyculture & Food Sovereignty

## Goal

Research the intersection of robotics and regenerative/healthy food production. The author's vision: CEE produces healthy food at scale using robots that enable polyculture (mixed crops, animals between crops), reduce pesticides, and prevent monoculture degradation.

## Context from Author

"I want EU/CEE to produce healthy food. With robotization we can avoid food degradation and introduce less monocultural farming. Interleaved agriculture — different small plots, different crops, animals between them, robots managing all of it. Reduce pesticides, separate crops with appropriate plants. The demographic crisis means fewer people to work farms — robots become essential. Also: food sovereignty matters geopolitically."

## Questions You MUST Answer

**Current state of agricultural robotics:**
- What agricultural robots exist today? (list top 10 by function: harvesting, weeding, planting, spraying, monitoring)
- What is TRL (Technology Readiness Level) for each?
- Cost per robot / cost per hectare vs. conventional farming
- Adoption rate in EU (which countries lead, which lag)

**Polyculture & precision agriculture:**
- What is precision agriculture? Current adoption rate in EU
- Can robots enable polyculture at scale? (evidence, pilot farms)
- What is "intercropping" and what are yield results vs. monoculture?
- What does regenerative agriculture achieve in soil health? (measurable outcomes)
- Agroforestry + robotics: any experiments?

**Pesticide reduction:**
- Current EU pesticide use per hectare (by crop type, by country)
- Farm Biodiversity Strategy target: 50% pesticide reduction by 2030 — realistic?
- Mechanical weeding vs. herbicide: robot performance data
- Precision spraying: reduction achieved in practice?

**Food quality & monoculture:**
- Is monoculture degrading nutritional value of food? (research data)
- How has wheat protein content changed over 50 years?
- What does biodiversity of crops do for human nutrition?

**CEE specifics:**
- What is Poland/Czech/Hungarian agricultural robotics adoption?
- What % of EU food is produced in CEE?
- CAP reform and CEE farmers — what's the current subsidy situation?

**Geopolitical angle (food sovereignty):**
- What % of EU food is imported from outside EU?
- Which crops are EU dependent on imports for?
- Russian war impact on food prices in EU (2022-2025 data)

If data unavailable: "Unknown. No public data available as of 2026."
