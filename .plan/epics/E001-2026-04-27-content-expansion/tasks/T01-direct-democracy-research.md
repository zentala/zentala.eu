# E001-T01: Research — Swiss Direct Democracy & EU Referendum Models

## Goal

Write a research file documenting how Switzerland and other countries run direct democracy systems — focused on frequency, scope, mechanics, and outcomes. This is the factual backbone for the Digital Democracy manifest page.

## Output File

`src/content/docs/research/t5-1-direct-democracy-models.md`

## Questions You MUST Answer

**Switzerland model:**
- How often does Switzerland hold referenda? (exact numbers per year, trend 2000–2025)
- What topics are voted on? (give 10 real examples from different domains: federal, cantonal, municipal)
- What is voter participation rate by referendum type?
- What is the threshold to call a referendum? (signatures needed, time limits)
- What % of proposals pass vs. fail?
- Are there categories that cannot be put to referendum? (fundamental rights etc.)
- What is the cost of running a Swiss referendum?
- What is the time from proposal to vote?

**Comparison with EU/national systems:**
- Which EU countries have referendum mechanisms? (compare: Germany, France, Italy, Poland)
- What's the difference between mandatory and optional referenda?
- What is "popular initiative"? Which countries have it?
- EU-level: European Citizens' Initiative — how many have succeeded? What happened?

**Outcomes quality:**
- Do Swiss direct democracy outcomes differ from representative-only systems?
- Has Switzerland ever voted to restrict minority rights? (the controversial side)
- What does research say about voter competence in technical matters?

**Digital voting experiments:**
- Has Switzerland or Estonia used digital voting for referenda?
- What are the security risks and solutions?
- Participation change after switching to digital?

If data unavailable: "Unknown. No public data available as of 2026."

## Data Sources

- Swiss Federal Chancellery (admin.ch) — official referendum data
- Swissvotes database (all referenda since 1848)
- European Citizens' Initiative database (ec.europa.eu)
- Academic: Kriesi & Trechsel "The Politics of Switzerland"
- Estonian e-voting reports (VVK)
