## Argument to make

Language integration happens through media before policy. People in the Netherlands speak excellent English not because of a government mandate, but because their TV was never dubbed. The EU can create the conditions: European co-productions with multi-language distribution, a pan-EU public media platform (BBC-scale but European), incentives for original English-language European content. Cultural integration is not imposition — it's building shared infrastructure for shared stories. Use the English proficiency correlation data from T1.4 research and the UK Open University / digital distance learning precedents from T3.1.
