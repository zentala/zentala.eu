# E001-T05: Research — Smart Homes & Elderly Care Logistics

## Goal

Research smart home tech and autonomous logistics systems for elderly people living at home. The existing care robotics research (T2.4a) covers institutional care — this fills the gap for aging-in-place: smart homes, food delivery, autonomous meal preparation, and supply chains for the elderly.

## Context from Author

"Robots can grow food for elderly and deliver it, maybe prepare it too. Smart homes in some way. Not just individual robots — you need a whole logistics chain: growing food, delivering it, preparing it. Elderly care logistics. Most important: cheap food via automation + cheap healthcare via AI/predictive medicine + supplementation."

## Questions You MUST Answer

**Smart home tech for elderly (aging-in-place):**
- What smart home systems exist specifically for elderly? (fall detection, medication reminders, health monitoring)
- What is adoption rate in EU? (% of elderly using smart home tech, by country)
- What is cost of installing a smart home system for elderly person?
- Evidence: does smart home tech delay institutionalization? By how long?
- Privacy concerns — surveillance vs. safety tradeoff: what does research say?

**Autonomous food delivery:**
- Current autonomous delivery services for food — which cities, which operators?
- Cost per delivery: autonomous vs. human driver
- Last-mile delivery to homes of mobility-impaired elderly: technical challenges
- Timeline to widespread autonomous food delivery in EU cities?
- Rural areas: drones for food delivery to elderly? Any experiments?

**Automated meal preparation:**
- Robotic kitchen systems — what exists? (Moley Robotics, others)
- Cost, TRL, timeline to viability?
- Community kitchen model: central cooking + autonomous delivery vs. in-home robots?
- Nutrition monitoring — can smart systems track what elderly person eats?

**Elderly care logistics supply chain:**
- What is the full logistics chain for independent elderly living?
  (food production → processing → delivery → preparation → medication → monitoring → emergency response)
- Which links are already automated / near automation?
- What is the cost of full in-home elderly care vs. nursing home? (per person per year)
- Japan model for elderly logistics: what is Uber-style care matching?

**Economic argument:**
- Current cost of nursing home in Germany, Netherlands, Poland (per person per year)
- Cost of smart home + autonomous delivery equivalent
- What is the break-even point?
- How many elderly would choose to stay home if logistics were solved?

If data unavailable: "Unknown. No public data available as of 2026."
