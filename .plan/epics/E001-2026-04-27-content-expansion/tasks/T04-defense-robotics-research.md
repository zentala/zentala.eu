# E001-T04: Research — Defense Robotics & EU Strategic Autonomy

## Goal

Research how robotics and autonomous systems change warfare, with focus on EU defense autonomy in context of Russia threat and US withdrawal. This is a topic the author explicitly says is missing from the current manifest: "robotization is needed to win the long-term competition with Russia."

## Context from Author

"Robotization is needed for defense against Russia. US is withdrawing from Europe. We need to ensure security. Parallel tracks: defense robotics, agricultural robotics for food security, care robotics for demographics. ReArm Europe is happening — €800B committed. The Ukraine war is a live laboratory for autonomous weapons."

## Questions You MUST Answer

**Ukraine war as robotics laboratory:**
- What autonomous systems are being used in Ukraine? (drones, loitering munitions, ground UGVs)
- What is the drone cost vs. tank cost? (asymmetric economics)
- What is Ukraine's domestic drone production capacity (2024–2025)?
- What lessons has NATO drawn from Ukraine for future warfare?

**EU defense industrial base:**
- What is the ReArm Europe plan? (€800B — what does it fund specifically?)
- Which EU countries produce defense robots/drones? (Poland, Germany, France, Estonia MILREM)
- What is current EU defense R&D spend vs. US? (gap analysis)
- What is PESCO (Permanent Structured Cooperation) — what autonomous systems projects?
- EU Defense Industrial Strategy (EDIS, 2024) — what does it say about robotics?

**Autonomous weapons systems:**
- What is "lethal autonomous weapons systems" (LAWS) — current status, UN debates
- What systems exist at TRL 7+? (examples: KARGU-2, Harpy, Harop)
- US autonomous systems: what is deployed? (Predator, Reaper, MUTT, etc.)
- Russia autonomous weapons: what do we know?

**Geopolitical context:**
- US withdrawal signals: what has changed since 2024 re: NATO commitment?
- What % of EU defense currently depends on US capabilities?
- Baltic states + Poland defense spending trend (2022–2026)
- What is the realistic timeline for EU strategic autonomy in defense?

**EU civilian-military tech transfer:**
- Can logistics robots be dual-use? (civilian delivery → military supply)
- Can agricultural drones be dual-use? (crop spraying → surveillance/attack)
- EU's position on dual-use technology development?
