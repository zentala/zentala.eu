## Steps

1. Read §7 in full and the DESIGN.md `term-tooltip` contract. Data source is the glossary collection only; the card is built at build time (`term`, `definition`, `href`, `status`, `source`).
2. `TermRef.astro`: renders `<a class="term" href="{href}" data-term="{slug}">` + the card (`popover="manual"`, Popover API, ~40-line script: 650 ms hover delay, 300 ms leave grace, keyboard focus, `Esc`, touch first-tap opens / second-tap follows, flip to stay in viewport, reduced-motion = no fade, no-JS = plain link; `aria-describedby` only). `needs-definition` renders the fixed sentence and the link; `external` renders the definition plus "Defined outside this site" and the source.
3. `rehype-glossary-terms.mjs`: first occurrence per page of a `term` or `alias` of an entry with `autolink: true`; skips headings, code, links, blockquotes, `kind: manifesto` entries, the `/glossary` page, and the page whose slug equals the entry's `href` path. Same output as `TermRef`.
4. `/glossary`: every entry grouped by the layer of its `href` (map href → chapter → `layer`; top-level hrefs under "Pages"), `status` visible, body long-form when present, count of terms in the heading.
5. Delete the `/tags` pages; add the two redirect rows; schema changes; `just build`.
6. Verification script in the scratchpad with Playwright (`chromium.launch({ channel: 'chrome' })`): open `/book/the-total-state/`, hover the first `a.term` for 800 ms, assert a visible `[popover]` card with the term heading; press `Esc`, assert hidden. Both themes; zero console errors.
