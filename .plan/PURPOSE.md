# Purpose of EU Reform Vision

## What this site is

EU Reform Vision is a public working project about practical European integration. It connects political goals with institutional, digital and industrial proposals that can be explained, tested and improved.

The site is not an official EU publication, a party programme or a finished policy package. It is a structured proposal space: clear enough for readers, concrete enough for collaborators and open enough to be challenged.

## Who it serves

- Europeans who want to understand what deeper integration could change in daily life;
- people building public digital services, businesses and industrial capabilities across borders;
- researchers, policymakers and organisers looking for testable European-scale ideas;
- future contributors who need to see the reasoning, assumptions and next steps.

## Editorial promise

Every public proposal should state its purpose, scope, expected beneficiaries, risks and next step. Supporting research may remain in a matching sidecar until it is stable enough for publication.

## Information architecture

The public tree is:

1. Overview — the thesis in one screen;
2. Why — the problems that require European-scale action;
3. Vision — the three pillars;
4. Principles — constraints and distribution of benefits;
5. Book — detailed proposals and implementation documents;
6. Benefits — consequences for citizens, businesses, regions and institutions;
7. Research and follow-ups — evidence, unresolved decisions and invitations to work.

The internal planning tree is separate from the public tree and lives under `.plan/`.
